The Home

Room to spread out.

Through the gate at Park Creek and down a quiet private street, a brick and stone home on 0.61 level acres. The two-story great room has hardwoods, coffered ceilings, detailed millwork and a fireplace. The kitchen centers on a big island with granite and gas cooking, opening to a sunny breakfast nook and a family room with a second fireplace. Out back, a stone patio steps down to a fenced, irrigated lawn.

01

Main-level primary

The primary suite sits on the first floor with a large ensuite bath, soaking tub and a walk-in closet.

02

A 17 x 32 bonus room

Upstairs, two bedrooms, two full baths and an oversized bonus room for a theater, gym or playroom.

03

The big stuff, done

2019 roof, HVAC from 2019 and 2025, a gas tankless water heater and motorized blinds in the great room.
